Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service free to use?

Yes, 100% free with no watermarks and no hidden fees.

Are my uploaded videos safe and private?

Yes. All uploads are set to Private visibility by default, meaning nobody except you can watch them until you choose to change the privacy status on YouTube.

How do I set the title and description?

Simply include a text caption when sending or forwarding your video. The first line is used as the title, and any additional lines become the description.

How can I disconnect my account?

You can send /disconnect at any time in the bot chat to immediately revoke access and clear your connected channel.
