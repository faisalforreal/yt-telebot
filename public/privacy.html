Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SendToYT Bot connects to your YouTube account solely to upload videos you forward or send to the bot. We access only the permissions you explicitly grant via Google OAuth (specifically video upload permissions).

2. How We Use Information

We do not read your channel history, private videos, analytics, or personal profile data. We only use your upload authorization to transfer the videos you send directly to your YouTube channel as private videos.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

You can disconnect at any time by typing /disconnect in the Telegram bot chat. Doing so immediately revokes authorization and clears your data.
